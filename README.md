## 🛡️ About Me

I work as a **Security Operations Center (SOC) analyst**. My day-to-day is monitoring alerts, triaging incidents, and deciding what is a real threat and what is noise. I also support **vulnerability management**: validating scanner findings, tracking remediation, and keeping reports accurate and on time.

I like turning repetitive security work into small, reliable automations so more time goes to actual analysis.

## 🎯 What I Focus On

| Area | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Alert triage & incident analysis** | Investigating alerts with the 5W1H approach and deciding to escalate or close |
| **Vulnerability management** | Validating findings, CVE triage, and re-checking fixes |
| **Security automation** | Python scripts and workflows that cut manual reporting effort |
| **Reporting** | Clear, structured reports for both technical and non-technical readers |
